# ENKU licensing

ENKU uses different licenses for different kinds of project material.

## Software and firmware — Apache-2.0

Original ENKU software source, including the firmware under `firmware/` and project-owned implementation tooling, is licensed under the **Apache License 2.0**.

The full license text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Apache-2.0 was selected as the main software license because it is permissive, OSI-approved, allows commercial and non-commercial reuse, and includes an explicit patent license from contributors.

## Documentation — CC BY 4.0

Original ENKU documentation, including project-authored material under `docs/`, `README.md` and `ROADMAP.md`, is licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** unless a file states otherwise.

License:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

This permits reuse and modification, including commercial use, with attribution.

## Hardware and mechanical source — CERN-OHL-P-2.0

Future project-owned hardware design sources and enclosure/mechanical source files published under `hardware/` and `enclosure/` are intended to use the **CERN Open Hardware Licence Version 2 — Permissive (CERN-OHL-P-2.0)** unless a file states otherwise.

License:
https://cern-ohl.web.cern.ch/

The permissive variant was selected to allow people and companies to study, modify, manufacture and redistribute ENKU-derived hardware without a reciprocal-source requirement.

## Branding

The ENKU name and ENKU logo are **not granted for unrestricted reuse** by the software/documentation licenses above.

The logo at `assets/branding/enku-logo.svg` remains a project branding asset. Reasonable nominative use to refer to the ENKU project is not the same as permission to present a modified device/project as an official ENKU product.

## Design assets and historical boards

Project-authored design assets may be released under CC BY 4.0 after the asset review is complete.

Historical boards and third-party material are excluded from any blanket license grant unless explicitly marked. See [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).

## Third-party material

Third-party dependencies, fonts, icons, example content and other externally sourced assets remain under their respective licenses.

Nothing in ENKU's licensing overrides third-party terms.

## SPDX identifiers

- Software/firmware: `Apache-2.0`
- Documentation: `CC-BY-4.0`
- Hardware/mechanical: `CERN-OHL-P-2.0`
